From Euler to AI:
269 years of fluid equations

On 8 September 2026 OpenAI released two papers constructing smooth solutions of the three-dimensional incompressible Navier–Stokes and Euler equations that blow up in finite time, with Lean 4 formalizations. The Navier–Stokes paper addresses alternatives (C) and (D) of the Clay Mathematics Institute’s Millennium Prize Problem.

That step rests on 269 years of human work: from Euler writing down the equations of fluid motion in 1757, through Navier, Stokes and Leray, to the mathematicians still pushing on the problem today. Together the two papers list 51 references. We traced every one of them and the people behind it.
