On Whose Shoulders · 巨人之肩中文

Every AI breakthrough
stands on human shoulders

When AI proves a theorem or settles an open problem, the ideas, methods and tools it uses come from generations of scientists. We go through the references of AI results entry by entry, find every name, and keep the record field by field, updated with each breakthrough.

Scientists are the bridge: one end rests on centuries of human knowledge, the other on today’s AI. This project is a tribute to the human scientists of the AI era.
